## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) defines the rules governing appropriate use of NovaGrid Technologies Ltd's ("the Company") information systems, devices, networks, and data by anyone granted access to them. It exists to protect the Company, its employees, and its customers from risks including malware infection, data loss, legal liability, and reputational damage arising from misuse of Company assets.

This policy operates as a subordinate document to the Company's Information Security Policy.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees, contractors, interns, and temporary staff.
- All Company-owned or Company-managed devices (laptops, mobile phones, servers) and any personal device used to access Company systems or data (BYOD).
- All Company systems, applications, networks, and data, whether accessed on-site, remotely, or via cloud services.

## 3. Policy Statement

Company information systems and devices are provided for legitimate business purposes. Use of these systems is a privilege that carries a responsibility to act securely, lawfully, and professionally. All users are expected to exercise good judgment and comply with this policy at all times.

Limited, reasonable personal use of Company systems (e.g., checking personal email) is generally permitted provided it does not interfere with job performance, consume excessive resources, or violate this policy or any other Company policy.

## 5. Acceptable Use Requirements

### 5.1 General Use

- Company systems must be used primarily for authorized business purposes.
- Users must not attempt to bypass, disable, or circumvent security controls (e.g., antivirus, firewalls, content filtering, MFA).
- Users must lock or log off devices when unattended and use strong, unique passwords or passphrases in line with the Access Control Policy.
- Software must only be installed from approved sources; unauthorized or unlicensed software is prohibited on Company devices.

### 5.2 Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

- Access, store, or transmit illegal content, or content that is discriminatory, harassing, or offensive.
- Use Company systems to engage in activity that violates any law or regulation.
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to systems, data, or accounts belonging to the Company or third parties.
- Disable or interfere with security monitoring, logging, or endpoint protection tools.
- Share login credentials, MFA devices, or access tokens with anyone else.
- Use Company email or messaging systems for unauthorized bulk communications, chain messages, or solicitation unrelated to business purposes.
- Connect unapproved or unmanaged devices to the corporate network without authorization.
- Use personal cloud storage, personal email, or unapproved third-party services to store or transmit Company or customer data ("Shadow IT").

### 5.3 Data Handling

- Users must handle information in accordance with its classification (see Data Classification and Handling Standard, referenced in the Information Security Policy).
- Confidential or Restricted data must not be transmitted over unencrypted channels or stored on unapproved personal devices or removable media without authorization.
- Users must report suspected data loss or exposure immediately per the Incident Response process.

### 5.4 Remote and Mobile Working

- Devices used remotely must have up-to-date security software, disk encryption, and a locking passcode/biometric enabled.
- Public or unsecured Wi-Fi should be avoided for accessing Company systems; where unavoidable, an approved VPN or secure connection method must be used.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT/Security immediately so remote wipe or access revocation can be initiated.

### 5.5 Monitoring

The Company reserves the right to monitor, log, and review use of its systems and networks — including email, internet access, and device activity — for security, compliance, and operational purposes, in accordance with applicable law and with appropriate notice to users. Monitoring is conducted proportionately and is not intended to infringe on reasonable personal privacy.

## 6. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract, and, where activity is unlawful, referral to law enforcement. Contractors and third parties found in violation may have access revoked and face contractual consequences.

## 7. Exceptions

Requests for exception to this policy must be submitted in writing to the CISO, documenting business justification, compensating controls, and a review date. Approved exceptions are logged and tracked to expiry.
